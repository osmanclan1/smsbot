Oakton Alert

Privacy Policy

Oakton Alert is an automated SMS service for tuition and payment reminders from Oakton Community College. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Who we are

Oakton Alert is operated by Oakton Community College (d/b/a Oakton Alert). We send automated text messages to students who have opted in to receive tuition payment reminders, deadline notices, and related enrollment status notifications.

Information we collect and use

When you opt in to Oakton Alert (for example by texting START or by signing up through the college’s registration or payment process), we collect and use your mobile phone number to send you automated SMS messages about your account, payment deadlines, and related reminders. We do not use this information for advertising or unrelated marketing.

Sale and sharing of your mobile information

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise transfer your phone number or SMS opt-in status to third parties for their marketing or promotional use. We do not transfer this information to affiliates, related companies, or other businesses for any purpose other than delivering the Oakton Alert service to you—including any transfer without payment, such as sharing with friendly or partner businesses, which we do not do.

SMS opt-in data and consent

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent (your agreement to receive Oakton Alert messages) will not be shared with any third parties for purposes other than delivering the service you signed up for.

We will not share your opt-in to the Oakton Alert SMS campaign with any third party for purposes unrelated to providing you with the services of that campaign. We may share your personal data, including your SMS opt-in or consent status, with third parties that help us provide our messaging services, including but not limited to platform providers, phone companies (carriers), and any other vendors who assist us in the delivery of text messages. These parties are required to use the information only to support the delivery of Oakton Alert messages and not for their own marketing or for selling or sharing your data.

Your choices

You can opt out of Oakton Alert at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can reply HELP for more information or contact us at the support email listed on this site. After you opt out, we will not send you further messages unless you opt back in (for example by replying START).

Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page. Continued use of Oakton Alert after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
